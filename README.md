# Generated TTRPGs

Generated TTRPGs, use at your own prerogative.

> [!WARNING]
> Every book in this repo was written by an AI, and none of them has been playtested. Read the [AI Disclaimer](#ai-disclaimer) and [Dead Dove: Do Not Eat](#dead-dove-do-not-eat) before any of it gets near your table.

## Overview

Four unofficial fan RPGs: Fallout and The Elder Scrolls, each built twice, once on *Cairn* (lean and lethal) and once on *Grimwild* (cinematic dice pools). This repo holds the compiled PDFs only, about 1,200 pages in all.

| Game | What it is | In the folder |
|---|---|---|
| **[Fallout 2e](Fallout%202e/)** | Fallout's wasteland on *Cairn Second Edition*: three stats, d20 roll-under saves, ten inventory slots and a short life expectancy, with rads, chems, scrap, power armor and settlements bolted on. | 11 PDFs, 484 pages. Start with [`players-guide.pdf`](Fallout%202e/players-guide.pdf), then [`overseers-guide.pdf`](Fallout%202e/overseers-guide.pdf). The rest are catalogs, 39 vaults and 21 campaign frames. |
| **[The Elder Scrolls 2e](Elder%20Scrolls%202e/)** | Skyrim in 4E 201 on the same Cairn chassis, with races, diseases, crafting, faction reputation and Standing Stones bolted on. | 11 PDFs, 470 pages. Start with [`players-guide.pdf`](Elder%20Scrolls%202e/players-guide.pdf), then [`wardens-guide.pdf`](Elder%20Scrolls%202e/wardens-guide.pdf). The rest are catalogs and ten campaign frames. |
| **[Grimfall](Grimfall/)** | Fallout's wasteland on *Grimwild*: handfuls of d6s, d8 "thorns" that complicate the result, seven classes keyed to S.P.E.C.I.A.L. and over 250 perks. | One 124-page rulebook, as a dark [screen edition](Grimfall/Grimfall%20Rulebook.pdf) and an ink-friendly [print edition](Grimfall/Grimfall%20Rulebook%20%28Print%29.pdf). |
| **[Grimscrolls](Grimscrolls/)** | Tamriel on the same Grimwild engine: 23 classes (the 21 skills plus Vampire and Werewolf), ten races, birthsigns and standing stones, across the Morrowind, Oblivion and Skyrim eras. | One 156-page rulebook, [screen](Grimscrolls/Grimscrolls%20Rulebook.pdf) and [print](Grimscrolls/Grimscrolls%20Rulebook%20%28Print%29.pdf). |

The "2e" is Cairn's. None of these is a second edition of anything, and none is connected to any official Fallout or Elder Scrolls tabletop game.

## AI Disclaimer

- **The text is machine-written.** Rules, tables, stat blocks, setting material and flavor were all written by a large language model. The editions here were produced with Anthropic's Claude, working in Claude Code. So was this README.
- **So is the look.** The layout, covers, emblems and diagrams are drawn by AI-written Typst code. No image generators were involved, and no artwork from the games is reproduced.
- **The human part was direction.** I chose the settings and the systems, set the design constraints, supplied the reference material, and approved or refused changes. I did not write these books.
- **The checking was done by AI too.** The review passes these books got (against the base rules, against wiki dumps of the lore, and for layout) were run by the same kind of model that wrote them.

If you don't want AI-generated material at your table, this repo isn't for you.

## Dead Dove: Do Not Eat

The label on the bag is accurate. If you open it anyway and find exactly what it says, I don't know what you expected.

- **Nobody has played these.** None of these editions has been run at a table. Every number was balanced on paper, by a model, by analogy with the base game.
- **They look more finished than they are.** Covers, tables of contents, tidy stat cards: the typesetting is the most polished thing here, and it says nothing about whether the rules underneath hold up.
- **They contradict themselves.** The same rule or stat line is restated across player guides, bestiaries, catalogs and campaigns, and the copies drift. One review pass over Fallout 2e changed about 560 lines in 99 files. Among its catches: a vault of "999 men and one woman" with three women in it, a grenade that cost more to craft than to buy, and a flying boss that could not be killed. Those are fixed. Assume their cousins are not.
- **The lore is approximately right.** Names, dates and item details were checked against wiki dumps where a quick search could settle the question, and otherwise trusted to the model's memory. Invented details sit beside canon ones, unlabeled.
- **PDFs are all you get.** No sources, no errata, no promise of fixes.
- **The good parts are borrowed.** Where these games work, it's because Cairn and Grimwild work. If you want a game somebody designed and tested, play those. Both have free editions.
